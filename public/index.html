A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Built on clear
responsibilities.

Can a utility operate without its own employees?

That is part of the planned model. A utility can contract approved providers while retaining an accountable owner or representative. Staffing scope and qualifications must be agreed before live operations.

Are staffing services included in a software package?

They are separate unless an approved written offer explicitly bundles them. This preview does not quote staffing rates or promise provider availability.

Where do the package details come from?

The website and registration load published package versions and module inclusions from the database. Your preview subscription keeps the selected version and module snapshot. Private registrations and subscriptions are not exposed.

Can I sign in with Google or Apple?

Apple, Google, Facebook, and Microsoft are planned. Their buttons are placeholders until provider integrations are configured. No live account is created here.
